# Parça

Parça is a mobile and web property-market simulation for practicing continuous order-book trading.

> **Development simulation only.** Parça does not accept deposits or withdrawals, execute real securities transactions, or convey legal ownership. All cash, positions, orders, returns, and distributions shown by the current product are simulated. Simulated distributions are not earned investment income.

Real ownership and real-money trading are outside the current product scope. Any future move beyond simulation requires a separate owner decision, qualified securities and real-estate counsel, and approval from the necessary regulated partners before launch.
